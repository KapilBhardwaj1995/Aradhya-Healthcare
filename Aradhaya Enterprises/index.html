Since 2018

Made in Shamli. Trusted across India.

Aradhaya Enterprises began in 2018 as a two-line diaper unit in District Shamli, Uttar Pradesh. Today our ISO 22716 GMP-certified facility runs four lines — baby diapers, sanitary pads, panty liners and maternity pads — with an in-house lab that tests every batch for absorbency, leak-back and skin safety.

We keep the formulation honest: 0% chlorine bleach and 0% fragrance in the baby line, pH 4.5 balanced topsheets for women's care, and FSC-sourced pulp throughout. Twelve SKUs ship to pharmacies, hospitals and homes across India — cash on delivery included.
